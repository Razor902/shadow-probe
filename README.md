# Shadow Probe

© 2026 Curtis Ray Dyess · Crimson Rose

**Status: DRAFT / GATHERED — Curtis's concept, not yet declared standing.**

A sun-diving spacecraft concept: safely relay and harvest the sun's energy,
as a potential replacement for nuclear power plants.

The probe lives in the shadow of its own heat shield. At its center is the
Beacon — sensor and relay in one package. It reads the sun's characteristics
up close and sends the harvested energy on, through relay stations, to ground
receivers.
